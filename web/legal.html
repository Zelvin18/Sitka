Privacy & Terms

Last updated: September 2026 · Early access

What Sitka does with your data

Sitka records sessions and events that you choose to capture, transcribes them, and uses AI to summarize, answer questions, and translate. Your sessions, transcripts, notes and recordings are stored in your account and are private to you. Audio and text are sent to AI providers (such as Groq, OpenAI or Anthropic) solely to produce transcriptions and answers.

Your API keys

If you supply your own AI provider keys, they are stored only in your browser and sent per-request through this deployment's own proxy. They are never written to our database.

Recording consent

You are responsible for complying with the recording laws that apply where you are. In many places you must inform participants — and in some, obtain their consent — before recording a conversation, class or meeting. Event attendee pages display a notice that the event is captured and AI-assisted; as a host, announce recording where the law or courtesy requires it.

Attendees at events

When you join an event as an attendee, your questions to the AI are stored with the event so the host's system can answer them, and questions you explicitly submit to the speaker are shared with the host. Choose what you type accordingly.

Terms of use

  • Sitka is provided as-is during early access, without warranties; features may change.
  • Do not use Sitka to record people unlawfully, to harass, or to infringe others' rights.
  • AI output can be wrong — verify important facts against the recording.
  • You retain ownership of your recordings and content.

Contact
