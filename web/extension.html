What the card does.

Everything Sitca does on a laptop or a phone, from a card the size of a sticky note.

Records the whole call

Picture and sound of the meeting tab, with your own microphone if you switch it on. Saved to your library while it runs.

Live captions, speakers named

Every sentence with who said it. Afterwards, ask "what did Mr Kamau say about the exam?" and get the answer.

A private conversation

Ask Sitca anything about the meeting as it happens. The window floats beside the call and stays out of the recording.

Host on Sitca

A link and a QR code for people in the room or far away: they follow the live captions in their language, hear the room, and ask.

A microphone that behaves

Off until you say so. If you start talking while it is muted, the card taps you on the shoulder.

A recap the moment it ends

Recording, transcript, notes, highlights and your conversation, on one page you can share. Leaving the call ends the session by itself.

Nothing happens until you press.

Sitca is a companion you invite in, not a bot that turns up. That is also why Chrome asks you for one press on the icon before the first recording on a tab.

  • Not recording by default. The card only listens after you choose to record, and stops when you press Stop or leave the call.
  • Your conversation stays yours. The Sitca window is never part of the recording or the recap others see.
  • Microphone off until you switch it on. And it is one press to mute again.
  • Only on meeting pages. The extension does nothing on any other website. Privacy & Terms

Questions.

Why does Chrome want me to press the Sitca icon first?

Recording what a tab shows and plays is something Chrome only allows after you have pressed the extension yourself: the toolbar icon, the keyboard shortcut Alt+Shift+S, or "Capture this with Sitca" in the right-click menu. Ad blockers and download helpers never need this, because they never record anything. It is once per tab, and the card remembers.

Which browsers and computers?

Any browser built on Chrome: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Arc, Opera and Vivaldi, on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebooks. Chrome 116 or newer. It does not run on phones or iPads: Chrome and Safari on iOS and Android do not take extensions. On a phone, use Sitca in the browser at sitcaai.vercel.app, or join a hosted session with its link.

Does it join the call as a participant?

No. Nobody sees a "Sitca bot" in the list. It records the meeting from your own screen, so it works on any call you are in, even ones you do not host.

Does it turn on Meet's captions?

On Google Meet the card switches Meet's own captions on and reads them, so the speakers' names come straight from the call. They are kept off your screen so the video tiles stay where they are; a switch on the card shows them again.

What about YouTube?

Open a recorded lecture or a live stream and press the card: Sitca records, captions and answers on it just like a meeting, and the recap is ready when you stop.

Is my session safe if I close the card?

Yes. The recording runs in the extension itself, not in the card. Close the floating window, change tabs, work elsewhere; it carries on until you press Stop or leave the call.
