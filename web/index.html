Sight

It does not just hear the lecture. It reads the board.

Whenever the screen settles on something new, Sitka reads it and adds it to the record — so the equations, the chart and the table are part of every answer, not just the words.

  • 1Whiteboards, slides, documents and video calls, from a shared screen or a phone camera
  • 2Tables come back as tables, charts are redrawn, diagrams become boxes and arrows
  • 3Nothing invented: if it cannot read a value, it says so

How it works

Two ways in. One Sitka.

Attending something?

A lecture, a meeting, a talk — on a laptop, or a phone pointed at the board.

Start a session

Stay present
